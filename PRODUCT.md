# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Engineers, hiring screeners, founders and ecosystem leads who arrive from X, LinkedIn or Google. They
want to judge in about ten seconds whether Opeyemi is worth reading, then read one article end to end.
Priority when they conflict: operators and founders first, hiring screeners second, learners third.
Learners still get a clear path through the series.

## Product Purpose
The personal site and long-form technical blog of Opeyemi Stephen (Bangkok): a Lagos-based software
engineer, technical writer, education architect and fintech co-founder. Success is a stranger reading a
full article and leaving convinced the author understands the systems he writes about.

## Positioning
Operator-grade analysis of Web3 infrastructure and African fintech, plus developer education, written
by someone who builds curriculum and products in both. Articles read receipts (filings, code, protocol
docs) rather than announcements. Confirmed by Opeyemi 2026-09-29.

## Operating Context
Readers arrive on phones from social links as often as on desktops from search. Articles are long
(10 to 35 minute reads), code-heavy, and published a few times a quarter.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js 15 App Router, MDX articles in `src/content/articles`, Tailwind 3, light and dark themes,
deployed on Vercel from `main`. Article URLs carry SEO equity and must not change.

## Brand Commitments
Name shown as "Opeyemi Bangkok"; "Opeyemi Stephen" is the legal name. Satoshi is the current typeface.
The About page commits to "calmer interfaces with motion used sparingly". No brand color is defined.

## Evidence on Hand
About 40 published articles, a work history (Solana Foundation, Consensys, Chainlink, Kronos, Daneizo),
three written principles, a "Now" list. No testimonials, logos or metrics; do not invent any.

## Product Principles
- Reading comes first: every page serves getting a stranger into an article.
- Build for clarity before cleverness (the author's own stated principle).
- Motion is used sparingly and never hides content.
- Evidence over claims, in design as in the writing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA in both themes. Respect reduced motion.
